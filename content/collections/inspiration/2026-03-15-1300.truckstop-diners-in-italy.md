        text: "So, the next time you are planning a driving trip in Italy, look on the bright side, what may seem like challenge to be endured at first can be transformed into a tour of some of the nation’s finest regional kitchens.\_ Fees on the excellent Italian freeway (or autostrada) system are calculated per kilometre travelled, so breaking your journey won’t cost extra.\_ Check on your maps, but most rural exits on the autostrada have villages or small towns nearby, where it’s a safe bet that you’ll find a very good, and cheap, trattoria, particularly if there is a semi-trailer or two parked in front."
