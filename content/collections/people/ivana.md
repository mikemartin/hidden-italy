  Ivana grew up in Alberobello in central Puglia and still lives in the centre of town in her very own trullo house that she inherited from her grandmother.  She has a Bachelor’s in marketing and a Master’s in communication.  Since graduating, she has worked in outdoor tourism, designing and leading tours in Puglia and other parts of Italy.   She has a passion for hiking and spends much of her free time exploring the walking trails around her hometown.  She is a qualified Nordic walking trainer.  She spent several years living and working in London and a year in Australia, based in Brisbane.

  Ivana leads our Puglia and our new Basilicata tour.
