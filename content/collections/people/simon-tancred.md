short_bio: 'Simon first went to Italy in 1980 and fell in love with the country. He returned in 1983, teaching English in Milan until 1988. On returning to Sydney he worked at  University of NSW taking guided tours to Italy annually. He later spent two years with his family in Florence between 2003 and 2004, working for Monash University. Since 2006 he has dedicated himself full-time to Hidden Italy. He has had articles and photographs on Italy published in most major Australian newspapers and magazines.   His book Trails to Freedom was published in  2025, about a solo hike he did across the Italian Alps.'
